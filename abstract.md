# When AI Changes the Work We Sell — A Software Consultancy’s Perspective

**Steve Haupt · Principal AI Consultant, andrena objects · Germany**

## Abstract

Software consultancies are hired for difficult projects: to understand the problem, build systems that stay maintainable, and take responsibility for quality. As AI takes over more of the implementation, producing code gets cheaper. Understanding the problem and judging the result do not. What does it mean to engineer software when we no longer write the code? What is our contribution worth then?

Drawing on everyday work in a software consultancy, this talk offers an early view of the transformation facing software engineering. Our experience across client organisations serves as an early warning system, revealing emerging dynamics, partial responses, and unresolved problems. Core themes include:

- The changing value of engineering expertise as AI takes on implementation and decision-making.
- The consequences of human–AI capability asymmetry for delegation, responsibility, and trust.
- The tension between accelerating AI capabilities and the ability of professionals and organisations to adapt.
- The open problems connecting practice and research: dependable delegation, the value of AI contributions, and the development of engineering judgement.

## Biography

Steve Haupt is a Principal AI Consultant and Agile Software Engineer at andrena objects, where he founded and built the AI team. He is a TEDx speaker and has given more than 60 talks on generative AI. He helps software development organisations decide where to use AI, how to evaluate its results and costs, and when to stop an initiative. His work focuses on preserving the technical judgement teams need to assess AI-generated work and take responsibility for their systems.
